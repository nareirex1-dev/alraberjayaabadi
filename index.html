About Alra Berjaya Abadi

Alra Berjaya Abadi is an Indonesian coffee export company committed to delivering premium quality coffee beans to the international market.

We work directly with local farmers across the archipelago to ensure quality, sustainability, and shared prosperity.

  • Selected arabica & robusta coffee beans
  • Transparent export pricing: EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF
  • Worldwide export shipping
  • Long-term partnership with local farmers
